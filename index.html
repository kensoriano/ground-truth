What's under your address?

Type a US address to see your flood risk, how your soil handles rain, and what you can grow, in plain language.

Results will stack here, from the surface down: water, soil, climate.
Data: FEMA National Flood Hazard Layer, USDA NRCS Soil Data Access, USDA Plant Hardiness Zones (via phzmapi.org), EPA Combined Sewer Overflow Inventory, DC Department of Energy and Environment, Esri World Geocoder, Photon, and OpenStreetMap Nominatim for addresses; USGS The National Map for basemaps. This is a screening tool, not an official flood determination or site investigation.
